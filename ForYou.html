"DO YOU ONLY LOVE ME CAUSE YOU HAVE TO?"

Hi Kyna,

I don’t know how to start pero I want to talk to you talaga for the longest time. I want to say that I’m thank you that I had the opportunity to have you and be with you for almost 5 years. Imagine that, nagsama tayong dalawa for almost 5 years and we we’re genuinely happy kaya I’m truly grateful for you.

Medyo late na tong message na to pero matagal ko na syang pinaplano I just can’t give myself a good enough reason to chat you again. I know I just hearted your messages that’s because hindi ko pa alam anong sasabihin ko sayo that time. Nahirapan din ako with all that’s going on, and that made me realize how you make my life a whole lot easier just by being there. Your presence lights up a path for me to take. It sounds cringe, yes but that’s the truth behind it. Madami tayong napag daanan sa loob ng 4 years at hindi lahat ng yon madali. Meron mga bagay na ang dali lang satin, meron din naman mga bagay na madali sakin pero mahirap sayo and vice versa. Mahirap sa totoo lang. Ang hirap hirap lalo na akala ko magiging okay ang lahat kasi I have friends, eh ang problem ayun medyo naubos sila HAHAHAHAHA.

Sorry for giving up on us. Sorry if may mga plano tayong hindi natupad dahil sakin. Sorry dahil hindi ko na kinaya. Sorry dahil may mga bagay na dapat ginawa ko. Wala eh, siguro napagod na din ako. The other reason I wanted us to break up is that a question lingered in my head for a long time. “Sino ako kung wala ka?” and that’s the question I can’t answer. Kasi what have I really done for myself without thinking about you first? Ayun hindi ko masagot. Kyna bata pa tayo, and there is a world waiting for us, challenges we need to face alone in order to grow. Alam ko nabigyan kita ng mga magagandang aral kahit na hindi ako nagign 100% best of the best pag dating sa pagiging partner mo. Pero trust me I did my best.

Sa hinaba haba ng apat na taon nating pag sasama alam ko hinihiling mo sakin ay isang sulat na ako talaga ang nag sulat. This is me trying to give you the things you deserve that I couldn’t give during our time. So sana basahin and intindihin mo yung naka sulat I think eto dapat yung part na sesend ko sayo sa messenger AHHAHAHHAHA bahala na dba.

A lot has happened in my life when you left, madaming good pero sa tingin ko mas lamang ang bad hehe. Sorry if medyo may rant ng onti, madalang lang naman to HAHHAHAHAHA pero ayon na nga for the last year of our relationship there was something that I can feel changing. Somehow I can’t pinpoint what was, but now na wala na tayo for the longest time since we were together, I can’t pinpoint is because I myself is the one changing. I promise you, I truly loved you for who you are with all my heart alam mo yan. I just don’t know what happened and we became distant to each other and grew different ways even tho magkasama tayo. Alam mo yon yung feeling na yes mag kasama nga tayo physically pero mentally not? Ewan ko ang hirap explain pero alam ko alam mo yon kasi isa ka din sa naka experience neto.

So ayon, I just want to say my thanks to you how special you really are to me. And the things that I really wanted to tell you pero hindi na pede HAHAHAHHAHA. Super thank you talaga dahil sinamahan moko sa long journey na to, isipin mo yon simula highschool to senior highschool tapos start ng college. Kumbaga malapit na ma complete kulang nalang working stage HAHAHAHHAHA. If you wish to continue this conversation press 1 HAHAHHAHAHAHA. If you want me to get the fuck out of your life press 2.

With all my love, always,
